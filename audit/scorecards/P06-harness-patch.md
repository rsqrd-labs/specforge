| # | Dimension | Score | Evidence |
|---|-----------|-------|----------|
| 1 | Task clarity & specificity | 5 | Single, narrow job stated in the first two sentences (`harness_patch.py:9-10`): new files only, nothing else. |
| 2 | Role & context framing | 4 | Role given ("test architect writing a targeted patch"), but the prompt deliberately omits `ASDD_METHODOLOGY_OVERVIEW` and `PROFESSIONAL_OUTPUT_RULES` (imports only `SECURITY_AND_PRIVACY_RULES`, `harness_patch.py:1-5`) — a reasonable minimalism call for a narrow patch task, but it means this is the only core-adjacent generation prompt with no "produce only the requested artifact, no meta commentary" instruction; the "no preamble, matrix, coverage plan, or commentary" line (`harness_patch.py:17`) covers the same ground ad hoc, so the omission is compensated, not a live gap. |
| 3 | Structure & organization | 5 | Short, flat, easy to follow — appropriate for a narrow task. |

| 6 | Edge case & failure handling | 4 | Handles the long-harness case explicitly via `_harness_context_for_patch` (`harness_patch.py:29-36`: truncate at the `## Files` boundary, falling back to a hard truncate) — a sensible degrade path. | 7 | Constraint completeness | 4 | "Never modify existing files" stated once (`harness_patch.py:14`) — appropriately hard constraint — but not reinforced anywhere else (no verify-checklist, unlike P02-P05), so there's a single point of failure for the model to skip it under pressure. |

| 10 | Consistency with target model | 4 | Same as siblings — provider-agnostic, unverified per-tier. |

| 14 | Testability & eval coverage | 3 | No `harness/prompt_eval` grader or golden-corpus entry specifically targets the patch path (all golden-corpus references found were for full-stage generation and route classification) — this is a real, if narrow, coverage gap: the patch flow's "match existing harness conventions exactly" claim is untested by anything in the eval suite this audit found. |

**Top risk:** No worked example and no eval coverage together mean a regression in "match the existing harness's import/fixture conventions" (the prompt's core differentiator from just re-running the full harness prompt) would likely go undetected until a user notices duplicate or non-conforming test files.

**Verdict:** HIGH (functionally sound and safe, but the missing eval coverage for its one distinguishing claim is a real gap worth closing cheaply).
