**Top risk:** The `PRODUCT_NAME` interpolation is the single unwrapped source-derived string in an otherwise carefully wrapped prompt — low individual severity (one short field, immediately followed by fully-wrapped, better-defended content) but worth closing for consistency, since it's the kind of small exception that erodes the "wrap everything untrusted" invariant this prompt otherwise models well.

**Verdict:** EXEMPLARY (dimension 9's PRODUCT_NAME gap and dimension 3's block-ordering nit are the only real deviations from a best-in-class structured-output prompt).
