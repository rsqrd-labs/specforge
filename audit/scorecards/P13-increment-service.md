| 14 | Testability & eval coverage | 3 | No golden-corpus entry or dedicated grader targets increment-generation quality or the numbering-collision edge case; `_log_token_usage` (`increment_service.py:652-669`) gives operational token telemetry but that is cost observability, not quality/correctness eval coverage. |

**Top risk:** No worked example for a complex, field-heavy output format (dimension 5), combined with no version tracking (dimension 13), means a quality regression introduced by editing this prompt would be both harder for the model to get right in the first place and invisible to the telemetry that would otherwise flag it.

**Verdict:** MEDIUM.
