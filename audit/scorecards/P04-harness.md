**Top risk:** A cheap-tier model asked to produce dozens of complete, real-assertion test files in one pass is the highest-effort-per-token ask in the whole pipeline; the prompt's own budget-degradation section is evidence the team already expects partial failure here, but the *quality* failure mode (technically-present-but-weak assertions that don't trip "no stubs") is less guarded than the *coverage* failure mode.

**Verdict:** EXEMPLARY (dimension 12/14 keep it off a perfect score; recommend adding a grader for the four never-drop categories' actual presence, not just their gap-recording).
